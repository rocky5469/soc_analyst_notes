☁️ Cloud Security Career Roadmap
Complete Exam & Certification Guide

⚠️ CRITICAL: Course Certificate ≠ Industry Certification

Two types of certificates exist:

  • Course Completion: Proof you finished Udemy/Coursera. NOT recognized by employers.
  • Industry Certification: Earned by passing official proctored exam. THIS is what employers want!

→ Online courses PREPARE you. You MUST take separate official exams to get certified!

✓ CERTIFICATION REQUIRED SEPARATE EXAM NEEDED

Purpose: Industry-recognized baseline security cert

STEP 2: Take OFFICIAL EXAM to Get CERTIFIED

Exam Registration:

  1. Create account: login.comptia.org
  2. Purchase exam voucher from CompTIA
  3. Directed to Pearson VUE to schedule
  4. Choose: Test Center (in-person) OR OnVUE (online from home)
  5. Book date/time

Exam Details:

  • Duration: 90 minutes
  • Questions: 90 multiple-choice
  • Passing: 750/900
  • ID: Govt-issued photo ID required

Register: Pearson VUE - CompTIA

🎯 Key Takeaways

  • Online courses ≠ Certifications. Courses prepare you; exams certify you.
  • All exams are through Pearson VUE (except some AWS through PSI).
  • You can take exams online (OnVUE) or at test centers.
  • ID must match registration EXACTLY - double-check spelling!
  • AWS Security Specialty is your key to Cloud Security Engineer roles.
  • CCSP makes you multi-cloud expert - premium salary boost.
